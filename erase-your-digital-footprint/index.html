Frequently asked questions

What people ask us most before ordering.

Can you erase everything about me online?

No, and be wary of anyone who promises that. Official public records — court filings, property records, licenses — cannot be removed by law. What we do handle is getting you out of the commercial sites that collect and resell that information, which are the ones actually exposing your daily life.

Is this legal? Do you hack anything?

It is entirely legal and we never access any system. We work only with publicly available information and use the opt-out mechanisms these sites are required to offer. We are a private investigation agency licensed in the state of Florida, license #A290026.

Why should I start with the $20 report?

Because without it you would be paying blind. The report tells you exactly what is published about you and how much of it can be removed. Some people have minimal exposure and do not need step 2 — we would rather you know that before spending $99.

How long does the whole process take?

The report arrives within 48 business hours. Removals take 2 to 6 weeks, because each site has its own response window and some require identity verification. We keep you posted along the way.

Why do you need my date of birth?

To tell you apart from other people with the same name. People-search sites index by name and age; without a date of birth the report fills up with profiles that are not yours and removal requests get rejected.

Is my information safe with you?

We use your details only to deliver the service you ordered. We do not sell them, do not share them with third parties and do not build databases with them. Payment is processed by Stripe; we never see your card number.

What if my information gets published again?

It can happen: these sites refresh their databases periodically. That is why the final report includes recommendations to reduce reappearance. If you show up again later, message us and we will tell you what to do.
